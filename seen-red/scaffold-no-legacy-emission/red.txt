Root cause (ledger rows 160/161, item omega-hub-join-and-pickup-legacy-text): a maintainer's
freshly scaffolded world (~/w/omega) had a full legacy/ directory (led/pickup/asof-export/
distance-to-clean, all four shims) despite the repo side of the earlier legacy-led retirement
(design/FABLE-LEGACY-LED-RETIREMENT-SPEC.md, now archived at vestigial_documentation/design/
FABLE-LEGACY-LED-RETIREMENT-SPEC.md) already being honored -- the retirement act only replaced
legacy/led's CONTENT with a teaching-refusal stub, it never stopped bootstrap/new-project.sh's
separate legacy/ emission loop (a `mkdir "$PROJECT_ROOT/legacy"` plus a `for verb in pickup
asof-export distance-to-clean` shim-writing loop, sourced from bootstrap/templates/legacy-*.tmpl)
from running at every birth. The maintainer's verbatim ruling: "I refused to push until legacy
was *GONE* completely."

Fix: bootstrap/new-project.sh's emission loop removed outright (not merely disabled); the three
legacy-*.tmpl source templates (legacy-pickup.tmpl, legacy-distance-to-clean.tmpl,
legacy-asof-export.tmpl) deleted from bootstrap/templates/ (legacy-led.tmpl was already deleted
by the earlier retirement build); bootstrap/templates/pickup.tmpl's docstring and NOT-REBASED
tail reworded to name no legacy verb at all -- the unrebased sections are simply not yet rebased
onto the boundary path, citing design/FABLE-BOUNDARY-MULTIPLEX-AND-CLI-REBASE-SPEC.md §5 and
stopping there.

Re-run after the fix (this directory's live state): all six cases green, exit 0.
